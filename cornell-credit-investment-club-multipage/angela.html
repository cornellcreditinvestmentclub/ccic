Angela Xu

CO-FOUNDER & CO-VP

Angela is a junior studying Information Science from Beijing, China. On campus, she is the VP of External Relations for Diversity on the Street and is involved in Alpha Fund, Strong Women in Business, the Big Red Pep Band, and the Aikido Club. Previously, she interned at Young America Capital as an Investment Banking Analyst, and this upcoming summer, she will be a Summer Analyst at Blackstone. In her free time, she loves playing tennis and badminton, playing the saxophone and drums, hanging out with friends, watching movies, working on startups, and meditating.
