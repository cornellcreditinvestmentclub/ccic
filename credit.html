What is Credit Investing?

Credit investing is the process of providing capital to companies and other entities through debt instruments, with investors primarily seeking returns through (i) interest payments and (ii) repayment of principal. Credit investing broadly spans private and liquid credit. Private credit refers to privately negotiated debt financing provided by non-bank lenders, such as asset managers, insurance companies, and private credit funds. Many transactions involve private equity-backed companies and are structured as sponsor-backed financings. Liquid credit refers to publicly traded debt instruments that can be bought and sold in secondary markets. The global private credit market has grown substantially in recent years and is expected to continue expanding as institutional investors increase allocations to the asset class.
