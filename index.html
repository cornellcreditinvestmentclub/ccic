Cornell Credit Investment Club

Cornell's first and only credit investing organization, dedicated to teaching students the fundamentals of credit investing across private and public markets. We prepare members for recruiting and careers in credit through real case work and direct industry conversations.

Our Mission

With many clubs on campus, what makes us different?

Opportunities for credit investing roles out of undergrad have exploded in recent years, but currently no organization supports students interested in a credit career path.

We pair education with real experience through case-pitching and presentations. Members will connect directly with credit investing professionals and funds, ensuring they're ready for recruiting and internships in the ever-evolving industry.

The Basics

What is Credit Investing?

Credit Investing is the process of lending capital to entities with capital appreciation through i) interest payments and ii) return of the principal amount. Within credit investing, the primary channels are private and liquid credit. Private credit refers to privately negotiated loans made by non-bank lenders (asset managers, insurance companies, private funds), with many deals structured as sponsor-style deals. Liquid credit refers to publicly traded debt that can be bought and sold on secondary public markets. The global private credit market is estimated to have $3.5T AUM, with deployment expected to peak at $5T by 2029.

What We Do

How we train our members

01

New Member Education

A 10-12-week curriculum that builds the foundation of a credit investor by exploring the different forms of credit investing, how to evaluate a business through a credit lens, how to read the risk spectrum across debt tranches, and how to build a credit model. By the end of NME, members will have a comprehensive understanding of the credit investing process: from sourcing, to underwriting, to pitching to the Investment Committee.

02

Investment Committee

After NME, students will have the opportunity to actively be involved through g-body and the Investment Committee (IC). Members will be grouped and assigned to different companies, and will be required to draft a credit pitch (memo and model) and pitch to IC. Top pitch teams will then present to our industry partners.

03

Professional Development

We will ensure every member is prepared for the recruitment process through resume and cover letter reviews, mock casing interviews, and networking tips. Our goal is for members to feel confident and prepared as they approach the recruitment and internship process.
